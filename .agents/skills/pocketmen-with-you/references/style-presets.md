# Style presets

## hero-chibi
For people and human-like mascots. 2.5–3 heads tall, large expressive eyes without childlike infantilization, compact shoes/hands, clean toy-like or 3D-chibi materials, handsome/cute balance, accessories preserved.

## soft-real
For pets and animals. Photographic or softly rendered realism, real species anatomy, faithful coat/fur direction, natural eye geometry, distinctive iris color, correct collar/markings, gentle compact poses. Avoid plush or anime exaggeration unless requested.

## plush
For cozy mascots. Soft textile/felt/fleece material, simplified geometry, stitched but not text-heavy details, compact silhouette and low visual noise.

## capsule-creature
For original fantasy creatures. Create a unique creature plus an optional **original red/yellow companion capsule** with diagonal color blocking and a heart/paw core. The capsule can open, be carried, or sit beside the creature, but should not copy a protected commercial ball/device design.

## auto
Infer the closest preset from references and the user's words. Prefer `soft-real` for real pets when fidelity is emphasized; prefer `hero-chibi` for people when “帅气/Q弹/chibi/cartoon” is emphasized.
