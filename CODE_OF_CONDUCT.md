# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in PocketMen with You a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, respect privacy, and avoid publishing someone else's personal images without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, discriminatory comments, deliberate privacy violations, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the repository owner through GitHub's available contact or security-reporting channels. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted in spirit from Contributor Covenant 2.1, shortened for this repository.
